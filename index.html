The room

Built for staying a while.

Limestone walls and warm cove light, long banquettes, marble tops and a bar that runs the length of the room. Doors fold back to the terrace, so the afternoon drifts in with the sea air.

Come for a quick glass at the bar, a long lunch that turns into dinner, or a Friday night that runs until eleven.
