# Surface: home page (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: locals and visitors equally, mostly on phones. Actions: book a table; come back on a particular weeknight; want the food and the bar.

Owner constraints (from feedback): no boxy UI (no outlined/filled rectangles around Book a table or panels); display type must read solidly on light grounds (no hairline serif); photos shown whole, never cropping off the subject; no tagline in the opening, the name leads; luxurious; mix of the venue's bright, colourful daytime and its dimmed, lamp-lit evening.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The Lounge changes with the light: bright Broadwater daytime at lunch, lamps lit and lights dimmed after dark. The page performs that change, opening in the light Paradise Point has right now and moving from day into night as it scrolls. It refuses the category's dark-luxury template (black, gold, italic serif) and the cream-and-serif restaurant default.

OWN-WORLD: Day: limestone-render grey ground (#e4e1db), near-black ink, Broadwater teal fields (#1d4a4f). Night: sign-black ground (#0e0d0b), lamp ivory ink, neon gold (#e2b866) for the one action. One sans family, Work Sans, light at display size, tight tracking. Pill buttons, square photos, no rules, boxes, glows or blur; separation by fields and tone steps.

STORY: The visitor sees the place itself, knows it's open today until a given time, sees the plates and the menu, finds tonight's reason to come, falls into the bar after dark, books in place, and leaves with the address.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed muted video, terrace by day / back bar by night, crossfading when the light changes. "The / Lounge" stepped across the lower left at ~12vw, white over a neutral scrim. Bottom right: one sentence (bar and kitchen on the Esplanade, Paradise Point), the live open-now line, and the gold pill "Book a table". Top bar: monogram + name, four links, Day/Night switch.
