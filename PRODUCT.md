# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Both locals and visitors, equally. Regulars from Paradise Point and the northern Gold Coast who come back weekly for the specials, live music and a drink; and first-timers staying nearby or passing through who are deciding where to eat. Most will arrive on a phone.

## Product Purpose
The website for The Lounge, Paradise Point: a modern Australian restaurant and cocktail bar at 44 The Esplanade, Paradise Point QLD 4216. Success is, in order: a table booked; a reason to come back on a particular weeknight; an appetite for the food and the bar.

## Positioning
A neighbourhood venue on the Broadwater with a lit, arched back bar, a fern-hung street terrace and a weekly rhythm of specials with live music. Brand mark is the gold neon TL monogram on a black oval sign reading "THE LOUNGE / PARADISE POINT".

## Operating Context
- Bookings run through OpenTable (restaurant ID 212261, to be confirmed by the venue). The site embeds OpenTable's reservation widget and links to the OpenTable listing as a fallback.
- Hours (from the OpenTable listing, to be confirmed): Mon–Thu 12pm–9pm, Fri 12pm–11pm, Sat 11:30am–11pm, Sun 11:30am–8pm. Queensland time, no daylight saving.
- Weekly nights (from the listing): Sunday oyster special with live music; Monday steak special with live music; Tuesday seafood platter for one, $33.
- Menu items named in the OpenTable listing (to be confirmed by the owner): oysters, scallops, Moreton Bay bugs, steaks including a 1kg T-bone, whole roasted chickens, fresh seafood. Scallops and salt and pepper calamari are confirmed by the owner's own photos.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step; deployable to any static host.
- No booking backend of its own; table-level selection is not supported by the OpenTable widget.
- Phone number: not yet known; must not be invented.
- Menu prices: unknown apart from the $33 platter; do not invent.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: The Lounge, Paradise Point. Monogram: TL in a gold neon double ring.
- The owner dislikes: boxy UI (outlined/filled boxes around buttons and panels), hairline-thin display type that reads like a cut-out on light grounds, and photos cropped so the subject is cut off.
- No "Pull up a chair…" style taglines; the name itself leads.

## Evidence on Hand
- Venue footage (bar, back bar, dining room, terrace, signage) and two dish photos (scallops, calamari): graded stills in `assets/img`, `photos/`; loops in `assets/video`.
- Drone footage, exterior photos and OpenTable confirmation are coming from the owner.
- No reviews, testimonials, press or awards on hand: do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles
1. Booking is always one tap away and never leaves the site unless the widget fails.
2. Real pictures of this place and its food lead every section.
3. The weekly rhythm is a reason to return, shown as plainly as the hours.
4. Nothing on the page claims more than the venue has confirmed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast for all running text; readable on a phone in bright daylight on the terrace.
